How It Works

  1. Upload Your Inventory to the Dashboard

    Add your inventory details to the Fulfil for Me dashboard and send the items to our Lagos fulfilment centre for processing and shipment to the U.S.

  2. Track Your Shipment to the U.S.

    Monitor your cargo from Lagos to its arrival at the U.S. fulfilment centre.

  3. Confirm Inventory After Arrival

    Once we update your dashboard with the received inventory, log in to confirm that all items are accurately listed.

  4. Connect Your Inventory to Sales Channels

    Link your Fulfil for Me account to your preferred marketplace or storefront for seamless integration and order management.

  5. Notify Heroshe of Sales

    When a sale is made, notify us manually, or the system will auto-notify us. We’ll pick, pack, and prepare the order for delivery.

  6. Deliver Orders to Your Customers

    We’ll ship the item, provide tracking details to the customer, and ensure the order is delivered successfully.

Some of the things you may want to know

We answered questions so you don’t have to ask them.

How can I send my packages from Nigeria to your US warehouse?

Ship your inventory to our Lagos fulfilment centre; we handle the freight to our U.S. warehouse and keep your dashboard updated the whole way.

How much does your fulfillment service cost?

Fulfilment fees are based on your storage volume and monthly order count. Reach out to our team or book a call for a quote tailored to your catalogue size.

How can I manage my inventory once you receive at your warehouse?

Once your inventory arrives, you can view live stock levels, update listings, and track every item from your Fulfil for Me dashboard, synced with your connected sales channels.

How do you charge for storage?

Storage is billed monthly based on the space your inventory occupies in our U.S. warehouse, so keeping stock turning over efficiently keeps your storage cost down.

What type of products do you accept?

We accept most physical retail goods, including clothing, accessories, beauty, and home products, excluding hazardous, perishable, or otherwise restricted items.
