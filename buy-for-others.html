How to Process Orders On Procure

  1. Browse and Accept Buy For Me Requests

    Explore the Marketplace to view unassigned Buy For Me requests. Accept only the ones you can confidently fulfill.

  2. Create and Send Invoice To Customer

    Send a clear invoice from the supplier to your customer within 24 hours of accepting their request.

  3. Confirm Purchase with Customer

    Once the customer approves the invoice and payment is made, share the receipt of the purchase with your customers

  4. Track Progress and Keep Customers Updated

    Monitor the process, track the order, and send updates to your customer at every critical stage.

  5. Earn Commissions for Successful Orders

    Deliver as promised, and get rewarded with commissions on every successful transaction.

Some of the things you may want to know

We answered questions so you don’t have to ask them.

Can I process more than one customer order at once?

Yes, you can. However, they will be required to fill a BUY FOR ME request, which you can accept and fulfill from your procure dashboard.

What is the percentage of the commission i earn as a plug?

Commission rates vary by order value and product category, and are shown upfront on your dashboard before you accept a request — so you always know what you’ll earn before committing to fulfil it.

Can I also add and manage my customers orders on procure?

Yes. Once a customer’s request is assigned to you, you can view, update, and track every order from your Procure dashboard, from invoice through to delivery confirmation.

How long does it take for me to receive my commission?

Your commission is released once the order is marked as successfully delivered and confirmed by your customer, then paid out directly to your linked account.

What do I need to do to be accepted to fulfill other customers?

You’ll need to complete your Procure profile and pass a quick verification so customers can trust you with their requests. Once approved, you can start browsing and accepting Buy For Me requests right away.
