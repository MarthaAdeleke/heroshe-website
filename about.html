ABOUT HEROSHE

We Are Opening Access To Global Commerce

The Heroshe Story

Heroshe started with a simple idea - What if you could live anywhere and access markets anywhere without being restricted by your geography? This simple question has led us on a quest to build a platform that enables our customers to live anywhere, buy anywhere, and sell anywhere.

It started with a few customers who were passionate about curating the best products for their local customers by shopping internationally while depending on Heroshe for the logistics to where we are today - helping merchants source, buy, and sell across the world.

We see a borderless future where lines that divide us will be erased by technology paving the way for commerce to seamlessly occur across borders. We are building for this future. This future is currently at our doorsteps. Many don't yet see it while we are already living in it. We shall usher them into it.
