How To Ship With Heroshe

  1. Use Heroshe Address

    Get your valid USA, UK, and China address when you sign up with Heroshe. Use it as your delivery address at checkout on your favorite online store, or have your packages sent there by loved ones.

  2. Track Your Shipments

    Track the progress of your shipment from its arrival at our USA, UK, and China warehouses until it arrives in Nigeria and finally arrives at your doorstep.

  3. Deliver Anywhere You Choose

    Deliver your shipment to multiple locations anywhere in Nigeria or pick them up from our Lagos warehouse for free.
