Some of the things you may want to know

We answered questions so you don’t have to ask them.

How can I use Buy For Me?

Simply create an order describing the item(s) you want, including links and quantities. We’ll source it, share an invoice for you to review, and handle the purchase once you pay.

What is the service charge for fulfilling Buy For Me?

Our service charge is a percentage of your order value, shown upfront in your invoice before you pay — so there are no hidden fees once you confirm your order.

Can we ship to other locations outside Heroshe Warehouse?

Yes. Once your item is purchased, you can have it shipped directly to your preferred address in Nigeria, or delivered to our warehouse first if you’d rather combine it with other orders.

Is the Shipping fee also included in the service charge?

No, shipping is calculated separately based on the item’s weight and destination. Your invoice shows the service charge and shipping fee as separate line items before you pay.

Can Heroshe pay for my items upfront, and I reimburse them later?

No, payment is required before we place your order with the supplier. This protects both you and Heroshe, and ensures your order is processed as soon as it’s confirmed.

How can i know the timeline for the buy-for-me process to be completed? and does this also include the shipping timeline?

Your order timeline (sourcing, invoicing, and purchase) is shown on your dashboard once your request is created. Shipping time is separate and depends on your chosen delivery method and destination.
